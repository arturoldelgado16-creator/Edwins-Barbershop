Our Story

Built one haircut at a time, on a simple idea: do right by the client, every visit.

The Owner

Edwin

"I didn't want to open a shop that felt like everywhere else. I wanted one that felt like coming home."

Edwin picked up his first pair of clippers before he was old enough to drive. What started as cutting hair for neighbors on a back porch turned into an apprenticeship, then a chair of his own, and eventually a shop with his name above the door.

Every client who sits in the chair gets the same thing: full attention, no rushing, and a cut that's built to last past the walk home.
